photo_library What makes a great moment

  • Real lab setups, apparatus, workshops, or project teams.
  • Good light, a clear subject, and as little blur as possible.
  • No faces if the people pictured have not agreed to be shared.
  • No exam papers, answer sheets, or confidential material.

shield Privacy, plainly

We never publish your email address. To keep the gallery fair, the service stores a random visitor cookie, a random browser device id, and a hashed cooldown key — no account required. Your photo and caption are public once published.
